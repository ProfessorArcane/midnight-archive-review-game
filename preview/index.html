How to Play

Select 2, 3, or 4 active guilds. The Herbalists, The Distillers, The Archivists, and The Alchemists keep independent scores. Teams choose a category and point value. Students write answers on the board when prompted. You control the timer and reveal the answer when ready. After revealing the answer, click the winning team to award points automatically. Used questions gray out on the board. Instructor notes are hidden unless opened.
Accuracy before speed. Recognize the pattern. Verify the details.
