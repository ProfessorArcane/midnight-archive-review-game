# Two student photos: content-to-game integration notes (ANONYMIZED)

**Status: Instructor review draft. Existing playable game and its questions are unchanged.**

## Source 1 — Student exam recollection
- One easy DEA-number/check-digit exercise.
- One business-math example: cost $30, sale $40; screenshot listed choices 10%, 25%, 30%, 40%, but the term *markup* was recalled. This must be repaired before use. **Markup on cost = ($40-$30)/$30 = 33.3%; gross margin on sales = ($40-$30)/$40=25%.** The recalled answer options include margin but omit true percent markup.
- One 150 mL / 1 fl oz bottles question with recalled options 1,2,4,6 and student choosing 6. **The rule MUST be explicit:** classroom 1 fl oz=30 mL => 5 bottles; exact US 1 fl oz=29.5735 mL => 6 to fit 150 mL. NIST lists both calculated and rounded values. Avoid ambiguity.
- Laws and federal-agency responsibilities were difficult; include realistic agency contrasts and scope-of-practice examples, not only memorization.
- Student said whiteboard visuals and pictures next to body systems helped connect anatomical organs to diseases and drug classes.

## Source 2 — Student whiteboard
- Urinary: urinary tract infections; possible mention of enlargement/hypertrophy; thiazide and loop diuretics.
- Respiratory: asthma, rhinitis, bronchitis, allergies; bronchodilators and -terol, glucocorticoids and -sone, leukotriene modifiers and -lukast, antihistamines, antitussives, decongestants. **Drug stems are mnemonic clues, not infallible guarantees; e.g. montelukast is a leukotriene receptor antagonist.**
- Endocrine: thyroid hyper/hypofunction, estrogen, insulin, sulfonylureas and biguanides; connect class ↔ brand/generic ↔ indication.

## Classroom/design implementation
Keep the full-screen board at six columns. Sprinkle these items into existing categories with subtle realistic anatomical diagrams/icons only when they do not give away the answer. Difficulty 100–500, instructor controls awards. Reserve pharmacy-business math and package-conversion questions until they are taught, e.g. PHRA 1009 and review. Keep all four guilds and their original colors. Do not publish student screenshots or the student's name.
